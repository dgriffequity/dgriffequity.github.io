About Move & Learn AI

Perceptual-motor development is the link between how children sense the world and how they move through it. Strong perceptual-motor skills support balance, coordination, spatial awareness, and confidence — foundations that carry into classroom learning, and everyday life. Storytelling and movement are used to introduce digital technology concepts in a screen-free environment. Dean's mission is to prepare young people with the necessary skills to be productive in a safe and inclusive AI environment.

Dean is a knowledgable educator and IT specialist. He has worked as a Senior Analyst Programmer for a global top-tier IT company and has acted in the capacity as the Head of the IT Faculty and Director of Staff Development and Learning at Melbourne High School. Dean has a Bachelor of Education with major studies in PE for the Handicapped and Graduate Diplomas in Computing and Mathematics. With over 20 years experience as a teacher, Dean is an experienced, dynamic and trusted educator who values an inclusive approach to the education of young people.

Our Programs

Digital Technology Through Story Telling

Dean has created original and engaging stores to subtly introduce fundamental digital technology concepts in a fun way in a screen-free environment.

Learning Through Movement

To assist in the wholistic development of the child, Dean's programs combine digital technology concepts with a tailored perceptual-motor program of activities. Dean's philosophy is to utilise movement to develop the psychomotor, cognitive and affective skills that may help prepare the young person for an AI future.

Safety, Responsibility & Inclusion

A human first problem solving paradigm encouraging the ethical, inclusive and responsible use of online technologies.
